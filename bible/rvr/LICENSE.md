# Reina-Valera 1909

The files in this folder are the Reina-Valera Bible of 1909 (Castellano), a public-domain Spanish translation of the Protestant canon (66 books).

- Source: [eBible.org, spaRV1909](https://ebible.org/spaRV1909) (USFM dated 10 Aug 2015; public-domain notice in the eBible.org `copr.htm` for this text).
- License: public domain (Dominio público). This is not the Reina-Valera 1960, which is still under copyright.
- Strong’s number markup from the USFM source was removed. The Spanish words, including 1909 spelling, are unchanged.
- Books 67–78 (the deuterocanon in this app’s English shelf) are not in this edition. The reader keeps those books in the King James text and says so.

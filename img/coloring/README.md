# Coloring pages

Original black-and-white line art made for Mind Soul & Body, one page for each story in Stories for little ones. The pages were created for this app. This project owns them. They are not copied from a coloring book, a website, or another artist.

`<id>.png` is the full page, 1200 by 675, in a few shades of gray so the lines stay crisp. When a page opens, the app turns the paper transparent and draws the black lines on a layer above the child's crayon strokes, so the outlines always stay visible and the eraser never removes them.
`<id>-400.webp` is the 400-pixel thumbnail used on the coloring book grid.
